PAWSIBILITIES RANCH DOG RESCUE

Every dog deserves a safe place to land.

Pawsibilities Ranch provides care, stability and compassion to dogs affected by abandonment, neglect, abuse and trauma.

Some dogs may be ready for adoption after healing and rehabilitation. Others need ongoing sanctuary care because a traditional home is not a safe or suitable option.

Daily food, enrichment, safe housing, medical care and patient behavioral support continue whether or not adoptions happen that month.
