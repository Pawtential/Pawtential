Data Deletion Policy

Requests may concern the Pawtential website or any of the four public Discord bots: PackTracker, Prize Park Arcade, Sticker Hunt or Yap-Caster.

What you can request

Depending on the service, deletion requests may cover user identifiers, gameplay records, scores, tickets, sticker progress, leaderboards, bounty histories, server settings and support correspondence, subject to necessary legal, security or accounting retention.

How to request

  1. Email PawsibilitiesRanch@gmail.com with subject “Data Deletion Request,” or open a ticket in the support Discord.
  2. Identify the affected bot(s), whether you are requesting user or server data deletion, and the relevant Discord user or guild ID.
  3. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens. We may ask for reasonable verification of identity or admin authority.

What happens next

We will review verified requests and respond within a reasonable time consistent with applicable law. Some records may need to be retained for fraud prevention, disputes, legal obligations or bookkeeping. We will explain applicable limitations when responding. Removing a bot from a server does not necessarily erase historical records immediately.

External services

Discord and payment providers manage their own records. Contact those providers separately for data held by them.
