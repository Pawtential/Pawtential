FEATURES

What can it do for your server?

✦ A new collection every month

The hunt features 12 collectible stickers per monthly theme, giving members something fresh to chase and show off.

✦ Surprise finds in your channels

Stickers appear in configured channels at unpredictable times. Members can race to claim a find before it disappears.

✦ Your own stickerbook

Track the stickers you have collected, spot what is missing and work toward completing the monthly set.

✦ Fairer hunting, less frustration

One-claim spawns, mystery finds and a pity-style mechanic help keep the hunt exciting for regular players and newcomers alike.
