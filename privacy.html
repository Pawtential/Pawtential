LEGAL & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2026. This policy applies to the Pawtential website and the publicly offered PackTracker, Prize Park Arcade, Sticker Hunt and Yap-Caster Discord bots. It does not cover private, internal-only rescue administration bots.

1. Who we are

Pawtential is associated with Pawsibilities Ranch Dog Rescue. Privacy inquiries: PawsibilitiesRanch@gmail.com.

2. Information used to provide services

Depending on the bot and enabled features, we may process Discord user IDs, guild/server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, relevant message IDs, server settings, game participation, results, points, tickets, rewards, streaks, leaderboards, sticker collection progress, user-submitted game answers and votes, and moderation or event actions necessary for supported gameplay.

Yap-Caster may process messages, replies, votes and game submissions in channels where its commands or games are used. PackTracker may read configured game/event messages to identify outcomes. Sticker Hunt may store claim and stickerbook progress. Prize Park may store game balances, transactions and prize-redemption activity. We do not claim that all bots collect identical information.

3. Website and rescue information

The static website can display approved public rescue dog photos, descriptions and stories. Website contact messages are currently composed in the visitor's own email application; they are not transmitted or stored by the website itself. If a form service, analytics or other integration is added, this policy must be updated to identify its data practices.

4. Purposes and sharing

We use data to run games and bot features, provide results and leaderboards, support configured rewards, respond to support and deletion requests, prevent abuse and maintain service reliability. Discord and third-party payment providers independently process data under their own policies. We do not sell personal data or use Discord message content for targeted advertising or model training.

5. Storage and retention

Server configurations, balances, reward histories, statistics and collections may remain while a server uses the relevant bot and for a reasonable period afterward. Logs and support records may be kept as needed for security, troubleshooting, compliance and disputes. No universal 30-day deletion promise is made. Exact retention schedules must be verified against each bot's production database before launch.

6. Security and choices

We use reasonable technical and administrative measures; no system is perfectly secure. Server admins can limit bot access through Discord permissions and bot configuration. Users and authorized admins may request access, correction or deletion where applicable using our data deletion process.

7. Children and international visitors

These services are intended for users permitted to use Discord under Discord's terms and applicable age requirements. Do not submit sensitive personal information through game prompts or contact messages. Applicable privacy rights vary by location.

8. Updates

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Contact us with questions.
